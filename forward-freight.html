How it works

From quote to POD.

Four steps. No middlemen. No surprise accessorials. You get a number, you get a truck, you get your freight where it needs to be.

01

Call or quote online

Tell dispatch the pickup, drop, weight, dims, and trailer type. We send a firm rate inside of 15 minutes — no fishing, no fluff.

02

We assign the equipment

Match the load to the right deck and the right driver. ELD log reviewed, hours-of-service confirmed, equipment inspected before dispatch.

03

Pickup & live tracking

Driver checks load, signs BOL, secures the freight. You get a tracking link that updates every 5 minutes — temperature too, if it's a reefer load.

04

Delivery & paperwork

On-time within the delivery window. POD uploaded same hour. Invoice issued — quick-pay options available. Done.

Questions

Asked often.

Quick answers to the things shippers and drivers ask us every week. Anything else, just call dispatch.

How fast can you give me a quote?

Inside of 15 minutes during business hours, and within an hour overnight. Send pickup, drop, weight, dims, and trailer type and we'll have a firm number back to you.

Do you handle oversize permits?

Yes — we permit and route across all 48 states, and we coordinate pilot cars and escorts where required. Send dims and route stops, we'll plan it.

How do drivers get paid?

Weekly direct deposit on Fridays. Settlements emailed Thursday night, line-itemed by load. Quick-pay available for owner-ops at standard factoring rate.

What CDL experience do you require?

Minimum 1 year verifiable OTR with a clean MVR. Less than that and we can talk about our mentor program — first two weeks pair-driving with a senior driver.

Where are you based?

Headquarters and main yard in Chicago, IL. We run drop yards and partner facilities across the U.S. — coverage in 48 states.
